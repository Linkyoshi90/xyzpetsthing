## Design thesis
**Order is a gardened thing, pruned daily with broom, brush, and bow.** Amatera is white plaster, dark timber, and raked gravel by day; lanterns, noren curtains, and neon-bright signage by night. The country's charm is the collision: a shrine bell that makes conversation lower itself, two streets from a gacha arcade.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Torii gate** painted the colour of sunrise
- **Noren** curtain hanging in a doorway with a single shop mark
- Paper lantern with hand-brushed lettering; the drum at second watch (curfew)
- Guild flags hanging from eaves; a public notice board carrying edicts, lost sandals, and found umbrellas
- Raked gravel and stepping stones in a strolling garden
- Woodblock print sheets hung to dry like sails
- Red cord tied to a parcel as a blessing; koi in a castle moat

## Pattern and ornament
- Family-crest (mon) roundels: single-colour circular emblems — the most useful item-stamp system in the setting
- Seigaiha waves, asanoha hemp-leaf, and shippo interlocking-circle repeats on cloth
- Woodblock print composition: flat colour, hard outline, dramatic crop
- Roof-tile end caps with a crest; plaster-and-timber wall grids
- Vertical banner (nobori) with brushed lettering

## Materials and finish
Timber post-and-beam, white plaster, dark ceramic roof tile, rice paper, tatami, lacquer, forged steel, indigo-dyed cotton, cedar, charcoal. Finish is **matte and precise** — swept, wiped, straight-edged; the only gloss is lacquer and wet stone.

## Architecture and silhouette
Layered castle keep above moat and stone, lantern-lit bridges, narrow lanes with noren and paper screens, a pagoda over cedar, theatre quarter alleys. Silhouette: **straight or slightly flared roof lines in stacked tiers** — flatter and sharper than the Lotus-Dragon Kingdom's deep curves.

## Dress and accessories
Wrapped robes with a wide sash, indigo work clothes, straw sandals, jingasa hats for the watch. Craft layer: soot-marked aprons, arm ties, tucked sleeves. Accessories: folding fan, ink brush case, coin purse on a cord, an oil-paper umbrella tilted low.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Woodblocks and ink, tea services, kettles and bells from Smith Street, ramen counters and stools, amazake on cold nights, gacha capsules, theatre masks and playbills. Signage is **vertical: noren, nobori banner, and hanging lantern**, hand-brushed.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 7`): **Akaname, Bakeneko, Blaze Cat, Kappa, Kasurenijimi, Kitsune, Momoshika, Shibari, Spider-Crab, Tengu, Yatagarasu, Yuki-Onna**.

The biggest yōkai roster in the setting. Design rule: **household object or animal, one impossible feature.** Bakeneko is a normal cat with a split tail and a lantern; Akaname belongs to a bathhouse; Kappa has the water dish it must not spill. Give shrine-aligned creatures (Tengu, Yatagarasu, Kitsune) crest roundels and paper talismans; give night creatures cold neon rim-light for the modern lanes.

## Guardrails
- Keep the daytime layer disciplined so the night layer can be loud. Both are canon.
- Straight/flared roofs, not deep curves — that is the LDK boundary line.
- Horror content here (the night school) should stay quiet and schoolyard-scaled, not gory.
