## Design thesis
**Harmony is policy, and it is carpentered into every beam.** Shenhedu's design language is bracketed timber, glazed tile, and the deliberate framing of views. Nothing is hurried: the river does not hurry and neither does good work. Order here is warm, wooden, and jointed — the opposite of Rheingard's stone-and-ledger order.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Upturned eave** with a row of small ridge figures — the instant silhouette read
- Bell and drum: bell calls dawn, drum settles dusk (and curfew)
- Gilt-character plaque hung over a gate or shop door
- Guardian beasts flanking the Celadon Gate
- Red cord tied around a purchase as a blessing against clumsiness
- Two-finger table tap to thank a tea pour
- Borrowed view: a garden window framing a scene deliberately

## Pattern and ornament
- Lattice window grids (ice-crack, cracked-ice, and geometric fret)
- Cloud scroll and wave borders on hems, rails, and item frames
- Dragon and lotus paired — the national device; dragon coiling, lotus opening
- Brushwork: signboards and labels rendered in confident calligraphic strokes
- Silk brocade repeats: medallions on a diaper ground

## Materials and finish
Painted timber with visible bracket sets, glazed roof tile, celadon and porcelain, silk, lacquer, bronze, rice paper, bamboo, tea brick. Finish: **lacquer-smooth on wood, glass-glazed on tile**, everything swept and slightly damp from river air.

## Architecture and silhouette
Twin drum and bell towers on a stone rise, a seven-storey timber pagoda, courtyard houses in grey brick alleys, humpback stone bridges over a working canal, a walled scholar garden of rock, pine, water, and pavilion. Silhouette: **stacked curved roofs with a single tall pagoda**.

## Dress and accessories
Cross-collar robes with wide sleeves and a sash; guild and rank shown by colour and badge. Scholars carry brushes, ink stones, and folded paper; artisans wear aprons and arm-guards. Accessories: jade pendant, silk purse, folding fan, tea set in a carry-box.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Tea services and timers, ink stones, woodblock and brush, lanterns, cricket cages, kites, paper goods, tea bricks (the Night Couriers' currency), sesame noodles, lacquered duck. Signage is **vertical carved plaque with gilt characters**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 5`): **Bambadger, Black Turtle, Jiang-Shi, Mirror Crane, Silkcoon, Sunwukong, Sword Koi, Vermillion Bird, Waterlil**.

Includes two of the celestial directional beasts (Black Turtle, Vermillion Bird) — treat those as constellation-scale and ornamental, built from cloud-scroll and flame patterning. Sword Koi and Mirror Crane belong to the canal and garden; give them lacquer sheen and brushstroke markings. Jiang-Shi is the comic-horror entry: stiff arms, official's robe and hat, a paper talisman on the forehead.

## Guardrails
- Curved roofs and bracket sets are the signature; if the roof is straight, it reads as Yamanokubo.
- Grey brick alleys are as important as red-and-gold temples — show the everyday layer.
- Keep motion slow and considered. Neon and rush belong to Amatera, not Shenhedu.
