## Design thesis
**Hospitality as a defence system.** Green hills and ring walls, bells at every doorframe, iron in every pocket. The design language is warm domestic craft placed deliberately against something in the mist that is politely waiting to be let in.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Bell at the lintel** — rings when a lie tries to enter. The most usable single icon.
- Silver branch over a spring (the founding myth)
- Rowan berries strung with iron nails at gates
- Ribbon-pledge tied to a council rail, snipped at dusk when the promise is kept
- Three larks (the bridge carvings face upstream, downstream, skyward)
- Chalk sigils marking true north; brass mirrors that catch glamours
- Bread and salt offered on a board (guest-right)

## Pattern and ornament
- Thick continuous knotwork and triple spirals — rounder and heavier than Nornheim interlace
- Hedgerow and bramble borders; oak and rowan leaf repeats
- Woven willow (wattle) texture on fences, hall walls, and basketry
- Muted fuzzy check on blankets and cloaks, never bright
- Initials carved on the *underside* of things (Bridge of Three Larks)

## Materials and finish
Half-timber and lime plaster, ivy, wet cobbles, willow withies, wool, pewter, iron nails, brass mirror, ash staves banded with iron. Everything is slightly damp: dark at the base, mossy in the joints, warm only where a lamp reaches.

## Architecture and silhouette
Ring wall with watchtowers, lanes of leaning half-timber, a triple-arched span, three living willows trained into an open-air hall. Silhouettes are **round-shouldered and overhanging** — upper storeys lean over the street.

## Dress and accessories
Wool cloaks with hoods, blue for Road Wardens, fastened with a penannular brooch. Layered linen under-tunics, knitwear, boots for mud. Accessories are protective: an iron pocket-charm, a sprig of rowan, a small bell, a stub of chalk.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Way-baker loaves, brass door bells, ash staves, ribbon spools, fiddles and pipes, shortbread tins, ale from *The Silver Branch*. Signage is **painted hanging boards** carrying one carved symbol — half the country trades in stories rather than coin, so shop signs are pictorial.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 26`): **Banshee, Bellmare, Boghare, Brasshorn Ram, Brownie, Dullahan, Kelpie, Needlehog, Rookmourn, Will-o-Wisp**.

The strongest fae roster in the setting. Design rule: **domestic silhouette, wrong detail.** Brownie is a helpful house-shape with too-long fingers; Kelpie is a beautiful horse with weed in its mane and river water running off dry ground. Bellmare and Brasshorn Ram carry the bell and brass motifs literally. Will-o-Wisp is the palette's cold green against fog grey.

## Landmarks to draw from
Green Crown (moot oak) · Rowan Gate · Bridge of Three Larks · Lantern Close and *The Silver Branch* · Willow Court (Courts of Courtesy) · Warden Walk · the Everything Store

## Guardrails
- Fog is a material here — always leave one edge of a scene unresolved.
- The "never call a stranger friend until sunrise" rule can show up as unsigned, unfinished, or unnamed things.
- Do not drift into Rheinland: Bretonreach half-timber is mossy and crooked, Rheinland's is ruled and painted.
