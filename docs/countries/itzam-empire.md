## Design thesis
**The city is both calendar and crown.** Days are read as shadows on stair edges, laws are carved as glyphs on stelae, water is counted as carefully as tribute. White plaster and straight white roads cutting green jungle — the highest-contrast landscape in the setting.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Glyph block** — a rounded square cartouche; use it for signage, item stamps, and UI plates
- Serpent balustrade whose shadow ripples down the north stair at equinox
- Cenote: a circular blue hole with rope ladders and shaded steps
- Sacbe: a raised straight white road with glyph-marked weight stones
- Cycle stone turned at the Binding of Years
- Clay beehive pots (honey is treaty and dessert both)
- Rattles and hand signs instead of shouting

## Pattern and ornament
- Glyph bands: rounded-corner blocks in red and black on cream
- Ceiba and canopy foliage silhouettes
- Stepped-and-scroll motifs (rounder, more curvilinear than ESD's blocky greca)
- Woven huipil bands with figured animal rows
- Echo/sound motif: concentric ripples, used for the Sounding Court

## Materials and finish
Limestone and lime stucco, bark paper, cinnabar and soot pigment, jade, shell, obsidian, thatch, henequen fibre. Finish: **smoothed lime that reads slightly chalky**, saturated paint, jungle damp in the shadows.

## Architecture and silhouette
Twin stepped pyramids on a plaza, corbel-arch doorways, roof combs, sunken ballcourt, shaded road niches. Silhouette: **steep triangular masses with a decorative comb on top**, against a flat jungle canopy line.

## Dress and accessories
Cotton wraps and huipils, jade ear flares, shell pendants, feather crests, painted body markings for scribes and runners. Scribes carry brushes, pigment pots, and bark-paper folds; water officials carry a marked measuring rod.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Codices drying on racks, glyph-marked weights, honey pots, atole cups, achiote-red turkey dishes, lime-cured fish with maize crisps, carved charms at the Canopy Relic Post. Signage is **glyph plus colour**, painted on plaster.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 10`): **Anomalocaris, Azureus, Ceibler, Motmora, Tapir, Turquoise Scale Serpent, Wayob**.

Rainforest and water. Ceibler (ceiba tree spirit) and Wayob (companion-spirit shapeshifter) are the lore anchors — give them glyph-marked skin and jade fittings. Motmora and Azureus carry the cyan/turquoise identity colour in plumage. Anomalocaris is the cenote-deep oddity; play it as something the divers do not talk about.

## Landmarks to draw from
Serpent Stair Precinct · House of Glyphs · Ballcourt of Banners · Cenote Quarter · Sacbe Gate · Garden Terraces · Sounding Court · Canopy Relic Post

## Guardrails
- Itzam is **limestone ridge and jungle**; Eagle Serpent Dominion is **lake and causeway**. Never mix the two silhouettes.
- Astronomy is the flavour: shadows, alignments, cycle counts. Put a measurement into every scene.
- Keep glyphs invented and consistent — do not reproduce real Maya glyph texts.
