## Design thesis
**Measure and memory keep the world upright.** Ankhmeru breathes in three beats — temple, granary, river — and every design should sit on one of them. Straight lines, flat planes, painted stone, and shade held like a lake inside a colonnade.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Painted pylon gate** with tall banner poles — the country's silhouette signature
- Hieroglyph band read as ornament (invent the glyph set)
- Ankh, scarab, and the winged sun as small-item stamps
- **Nilometer**: a stair and shaft cut to the river, flood height carved in stone
- Boat with a painted eye on the bow
- Linen wish-strips tied to hall railings
- Scribe's palette, reed pen, and tally niche

## Pattern and ornament
- Register bands: figures and text stacked in horizontal ruled rows
- Lotus and papyrus capitals; bundled-reed column shafts
- Checkerboard and zigzag-water bands on borders
- Star-speckled blue ceiling over a hall
- Cavetto cornice with vertical fluting and a winged disc

## Materials and finish
Cut limestone and sandstone, mudbrick, painted plaster, linen, faience, alabaster, copper, cedar, reed and papyrus. Finish: **flat matte paint on smooth stone**, sun-bleached outside, cool and saturated in the shade.

## Architecture and silhouette
Twin banks joined by barges and a lion-pylon bridge; east-bank temples with painted pylons; a forest of painted columns; west-bank terraces climbing to tomb roads in golden cliffs; palm-roofed market stalls. Silhouette: **trapezoidal masses and flat roof lines with tall thin banner poles**.

## Dress and accessories
Pleated white linen, broad bead collars, kohl-lined eyes, sandals, shaved heads or wigs by station. Priests carry censers; scribes carry palettes and rolls; Medjay wardens wear sand-coloured cloaks and carry ledgers and batons.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Flat loaves and barley beer, honey cakes shaped like lotus buds, alabaster jars, faience amulets, scarab charms, linen wraps, desert glass, reed skiffs, and the dyed saffron-and-lapis striped canvas of the Bazaar Tent. Signage is **carved and painted glyph panels**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 15`): **Anubis, Crocora, Ibiset, Landharpy, Scarabronze, Spinosaur, Wadjet**.

Animal-headed divinity and river fauna. Design them like **temple art stepped into three dimensions**: flat colour blocks, kohl-lined eyes, gold and lapis collars, poses that hold a profile. Scarabronze is the bronze-shelled beetle; Wadjet is the cobra-guardian; Crocora and Spinosaur are the river's teeth. Keep the palette identical to the architecture so creature and city read as one culture.

## Guardrails
- Ankhmeru is **living and painted**, not a dusty ruin — the tomb roads are the only "ancient" part.
- Invent the glyph vocabulary; do not transcribe real hieroglyphic texts onto items.
- Distinguish from Hammurabia: Kemet is dressed stone, painted registers, and flat planes; Hammurabia is mudbrick, blue glaze, and wedge script.
