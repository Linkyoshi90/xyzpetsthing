## Design thesis
**A broken bracelet lying in the sea.** Concentric arcs of pale basalt stepping down into a still lagoon, half-returned to the water that punished it. The design language is **geometry drowning**: perfect circles interrupted by tide, colonnades visible under glass-clear water, bronze going green below the waterline.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Concentric rings** — the town's plan, its emblem, and its way of thinking. Use ring geometry for UI here.
- Bronze-green pillar rising from water, banded with old glyphs
- Shell chime hung from a bridge on a still night (Bell Nights)
- Bronze bulls of the old harbour, salt-darkened
- Drowned bells that toll on their own when tempers rise
- Floodstairs with lamps lit along them; a witness bell for each dive
- The **Heart Mirror**: the central lagoon reflecting the rings so perfectly the town seems suspended

## Pattern and ornament
- Ring and spiral inlay in paving; radial spokes crossing concentric arcs
- Wave-meander borders (a drowned cousin of Aegia Aeterna's meander — deliberately related)
- Dolphin and bull relief carvings, salt-darkened in the grooves
- Waterline staining as pattern: horizontal bands of algae, barnacle, and bleach on every vertical
- Waxed-tablet lettering from the Drowned Stacks Library

## Materials and finish
Basalt and pale limestone, bronze, barnacle and coral crust, waxed tablets, oiled rope, glass floats, tarred boat wood, shell. Finish: **wet, cold, and encrusted below the line; sun-bleached above it.** Every object should show where the water reaches.

## Architecture and silhouette
Ring-quays and stepped terraces, half-submerged palace galleries, rope bridges and shell-chime chains linking arcs, narrow boats in old canals. Silhouette: **curved horizontals with broken verticals** — columns standing where a roof no longer is.

## Dress and accessories
Practical sea dress: oiled wraps, rope belts, bare feet on wet stone, diving gear of weight-belts and lines. Divers carry a witness token and a tally of breaths. Old-capital finery survives as salvage: a bronze pin, a cracked ring, something too grand for a fishing family.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Saltproof tools from Tideglass Aquatics, tagged `Aquatic` / `Pelagoric` / `Pelagora` / `Underwater` by guild rule — a naming convention already used by the shop. Also: waxed tablets, dive registers, shell chimes, glass floats, lamp cages. Signage is **carved into stone and re-cut as it erodes**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 28`): **Archelon, Conch Mail Turtle, Doggo, Mantarrow, Moopie, Reeffin, Sawfin, Urchskin**.

Heavily shelled and armoured sea life — the roster leans turtle and ray. Design them with **architecture on their backs**: shells that echo the ring plan, barnacle and bronze-green crust, glyph banding along carapace edges. Anything that lives in the drowned galleries should look older and slower than it needs to be.

## Landmarks to draw from
The outer ring and floodstairs · the Heart Mirror lagoon · Tideglass Aquatics · Drowned Stacks Library · Floodstairs Diver Guild · the drowned colonnades

## Guardrails
- The town is **half-drowned, not ruined** — people live here, on the high arcs, right now.
- Pride caused the sinking; keep the visual tone humbled and careful, never grand.
- No tropical warmth. Navakai is warm and open; Pelagora is cold, curved, and quiet.
