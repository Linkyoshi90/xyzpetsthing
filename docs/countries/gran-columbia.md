## Design thesis
**Contemporary, and proud of it.** Solvine is the setting's most modern city after Meridian Arc: glass towers over old plazas, streetcars, murals, licensed dispensaries with health desks and clear signage. The design language is **bright civic infrastructure plus hand-painted colour** — official and informal layered on the same wall.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Painted stairs** — a mural that only resolves when seen from the right angle
- Cable-car cabin on a wire against a hillside
- Vineyard trellis over a riverbank amphitheatre
- Harm-reduction kit: water station, info card, marked health tent
- Fruit pyramid at the covered market
- Coded stickers used by the Vine Syndicate couriers (a quiet visual thread for shadow content)
- Posted city budgets on a public wall

## Pattern and ornament
- Big-block mural geometry: flat colour fields, bold outline, one photographic face
- Woven ruana/poncho stripe bands for textiles and item trims
- Colonial tile floor patterns in the historic core (octagon-and-dot, cross-tile)
- Bike-lane and crosswalk striping used graphically
- Trellis lattice and vine as a recurring overlay

## Materials and finish
Glass curtain wall, painted concrete, brick, corrugated roofing on the hillside, terracotta tile, chrome transit fittings, printed vinyl signage, canvas awnings. Finish is **clean municipal plus sun-faded paint**.

## Architecture and silhouette
Riverwalk towers, a historic plaza with cathedral shadow, hillside barrios stepping down like terraces, market sheds, a civic-legal complex, start-up sheds turned studios. Silhouette: **stacked hillside boxes against tall smooth glass**.

## Dress and accessories
Modern everyday: cycling kit, work shirts, aprons, office wear, sun hats. Traditional accents appear as *chosen* pieces — a woven ruana, an embroidered hatband, beadwork. Civic Guard wear body cams and reflective trim. Accessories: transit tap card, reusable bottle, tote from Mercado Andino.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Arepas and empanadas, ceviche cups, coffee roasters, passionfruit juice, urban-winery bottles, saplings handed out on civic days, streetcar tickets. Signage is **modern sans-serif on enamel plates**, plus hand-painted vendor boards one block inland.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 19`): **Armadillo, Capybara, Curupira, Date Palm Beetle, Riverdillo, Slothren, Toucan**.

Real neotropical fauna with one folklore ringer (Curupira, backwards feet, ember hair). Keep the animals *actually animal* — this is the least fantastical roster in Verdania — and let the city show up as accessories: a bandana, a courier sticker on a shell, a market tag on an ear. Curupira is the exception and should look genuinely forest-side, not urban.

## Landmarks to draw from
Golden Mile · Cerro Verde · Plaza Sol · Mercado Andino · Juramento Complex (Health & Safety Pavilion) · Parque del Vino · Solar Yard · Solvine Plaza Kiosk

## Guardrails
- The country card jokes about cocaine; the actual lore is careful — **licensed, zoned, health-desk** framing. Keep art on the regulated-and-organised side, not the cartel side.
- Modern means modern: phones, cameras, tap cards, spreadsheets. Do not medievalise it.
- Distinguish from Xochimex: Solvine is glass-and-hillside, Xochival is plateau-basin and canal.
