## Design thesis
**Stone fitted so tight a knife finds no path.** Precision without ornament. Intirumi's beauty is structural — trapezoid doors, water channels that sing, terraces cut like green combs — and the only riot of colour is textile. Reciprocity (*ayni*) is the social engine, so design should show labour shared: stairs mended, water carried, cords knotted.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Trapezoid** — doors, niches, windows. Everything narrows toward the top; use it as the frame shape for UI panels here.
- **Khipu**: knotted colour cords used for counts and tribute records
- The noon-shadow pin and the light-line crossing a niche
- Stepped-cross (chakana-style block motif) in stone and weave
- Water channel cut into stone, running day and night
- Shell horn carried by road runners; way-station niches
- A bright cord knotted to a purchase as a blessing

## Pattern and ornament
- Stepped fret and stepped-diamond bands — angular, no curves
- Warp-faced weaving stripes: dense narrow bands, high contrast, symmetrical about a centre line
- Stone coursing itself as pattern: irregular polygonal blocks with tight joins
- Terrace contour lines as a landscape pattern
- Repeated niche rows along a wall

## Materials and finish
Dressed granite, adobe, thatch, alpaca and vicuña wool, gold sheet, copper tools, gourds, salt, coca leaf, rope-fibre bridges. Finish: **matte stone with polished joins**, textiles saturated and slightly fuzzy, gold used sparingly as sheet rather than filigree.

## Architecture and silhouette
Terraces stepping up a mountain, cyclopean walls, trapezoidal gates, a stone water stair, a road that divides four ways, a high ledge walk with low walls. Silhouette: **battered (inward-leaning) walls and horizontal terrace lines**, punctuated by a single temple mass.

## Dress and accessories
Tunic (unku) and mantle in banded weave, four-cornered hats, ear ornaments for rank, coca pouch, carrying cloth knotted at the shoulder, sandals. Officials carry khipu bundles. Road Keepers carry rope and shell horns.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Khipu cords, gourd cups, copper tools, salt blocks, quinoa and potato baskets, mate de coca, chicha morada, a gold-sided potato worth talking about. Signage is largely **non-textual** — knot, colour, and niche placement do the work, which is a useful hook for puzzle content.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 20`): **Amaru, Argentinosaurus, Condorune, Fishman, Pishtaco, Vicunero**.

Altitude fauna plus Andean myth. Amaru (double-headed serpent) and Condorune should carry stepped-fret markings and gold accents; Vicunero is the woolly banded-textile creature; Pishtaco is the dread entry — keep it human-shaped and wrong, pale and fat-hungry, seen at distance. Argentinosaurus links thematically to the hidden Aeonstep Plateau roster.

## Landmarks to draw from

## Guardrails
- **No curves in the stonework.** Angular geometry is the entire signature.
- Gold is temple-only. Everyday Intirumi is grey, green, and woven.
- Altitude is a design constraint: thin air, hard shadows, cold blue shade next to hot gold light.
