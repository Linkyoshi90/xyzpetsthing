## Design thesis
**Work bright, play safer, leave no trace but footprints.** Redwind is glass towers over a deep blue harbour with a surf beach a tram ride away. The visual signature is **safety infrastructure as decoration**: red-and-yellow flags, lifeguard towers, colour-coded bins, UV warnings — bright civic hardware against sun-bleached everything.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Red-and-yellow flag pair** planted in sand — the country's most recognisable mark
- Sail-of-shells roof over the arts house
- Steel arch over the harbour with a walkable top
- Ferry wharf numbers and the tap-on/tap-off gate
- Lifeguard tower, ice-pop kiosk, salt-stained chip shop
- Cockatoos and ibises with criminal intent around food
- Wide-brim hat, zinc, and factor-50 as an actual civic creed

## Pattern and ornament
- Diagonal hi-vis and hazard striping used decoratively
- Wide horizontal bands: sea / sand / bush / sky
- Laneway mural style: quick, loose, and layered over brick
- Corrugated iron ribbing on sheds and roofs
- Enamel transit signage and route diagrams

## Materials and finish
Glass, steel, corrugated iron, sandstone, marine ply, salt-crusted rope, sun-faded canvas, powder-coated rails. Finish: **bleached and salted** — colour on the coast fades fast, so keep the brights hard-edged and the neutrals chalky.

## Architecture and silhouette
Glass towers along a quay, ferry wharves, a shell-roof arts house, a steel arch bridge, converted boat sheds and brew halls, market halls, headland lookouts. Silhouette: **vertical glass, one great curve (the shell roof), one great arc (the bridge)**.

## Dress and accessories
Board shorts, swim gear, sun shirts, wide-brim hats, thongs, and business wear on the same footpath. Surf Wardens in red and yellow; Harbor Watch in navy. Accessories: reef-safe sunscreen, keep-cup, transit card, a leather hat you will not actually wear later.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Fish and chips in paper, meat pies, prawn plates, mango crates, flat whites, ice pops, oyster ice trays, Wheel of Fate carnival kit (`?pg=rsc-wof`), red-dust roadhouse goods (`?pg=rsc-roadhouse`). Signage is **enamel civic plate plus hand-painted kiosk board**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 17`): **Drop Bear, Emberu, Ironbeak, Kangaroo, Min-Min Lights, Sea Turtle, Wattlefox**.

Real fauna, one tall tale (Drop Bear), one genuine outback mystery (Min-Min Lights). Keep the animals **plausible and slightly sunburnt**: dusty coats, salt on shells, hard rim-light. Min-Min Lights is the abstract one — floating lights on a night road, no body. Emberu and Wattlefox carry the fire-and-wattle palette (ember orange, wattle yellow).

## Landmarks to draw from

## Guardrails
- Two climates in one country: harbour city and red-dust road. Show both; the roadhouse is not a different nation.
- Sun is a hazard here, not just lighting — hats, shade sails, and squint are part of the look.
- This country and the [Yara Nations](yara-nations.md) share a continent. Keep them visually distinct and never blend their design languages.
