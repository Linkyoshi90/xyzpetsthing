> This is not a country and has no culture of its own — it is a **wild region**, listed here because it carries a full species roster (`region_id = 27`) and needs its own art direction.

## Design thesis
**Deep time, still running.** A shelf of highland above Intirumi's terraces where extinct things never got the message. There is no architecture, no signage, and no people — the design language is entirely **landscape, weather, and animal**. Any human trace should look accidental: a rock slide, a rope left behind, a cairn nobody maintains.

## Motifs and symbols
- The rock slide that opened the route — raw broken stone against old weathered stone
- Cairns nobody has maintained; a rope frayed at an anchor
- Layered strata in a cliff face, read like a calendar
- Cloud sitting *below* the path
- Tracks in mud that are the wrong size

## Pattern and ornament
Essentially none — that absence is the point. Pattern here is **natural**: strata banding, fern spirals, pelt markings, lichen mottle, scree grading from boulder to gravel.

## Materials and finish
Wet rock, moss, fern, tussock, hide and fur, bone, ice. Finish: **cold, damp, and unfinished** — no cut edges, no paint, nothing squared.

## Landscape and silhouette
Stepped highland shelves above the treeline, cave mouths, fern gullies, boulder fields, a cloud layer that keeps cutting the view. Silhouette: **broken horizontals of rock with animal masses breaking the skyline**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 27`): **Anvil Tortoise, Argentowlis, Dodobold, Fernstalker, Gigantopithecus, Hairypterix, Rheintalensis, Rhiwool, Sabre, Smilodon, Squirricerat, Turtfolk, Turtworld, Tuskin, Tusktaur**.

The largest wild roster in the game — megafauna, extinct birds, and shelled giants. Design rules:
- **Scale first.** Several of these should feel like terrain: Turtworld and Anvil Tortoise are landscape with legs.
- **Weather-worn, not decorated.** No harness, no jewellery, no cultural accessories. This is the one region where creatures carry nothing made by hands.
- **Cold-adapted coats:** heavy fur (Rhiwool, Tuskin, Gigantopithecus), thick keratin, moss and lichen growing on the slowest ones.
- Predators (Sabre, Smilodon, Fernstalker) should be built for ambush in fern and cloud, low and quiet.

## Guardrails
- Do not give this region a civilisation. Its power is that Intirumi's perfect masonry stops at the cliff edge and something older continues past it.
- Keep the palette cold and desaturated so the Sapa Inti gold reads as a shock when the player goes back down.
