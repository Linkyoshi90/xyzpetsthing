## Design thesis
**Beauty is a public trust.** Scratch a pillar here and a priest-accountant hands you a broom faster than a fine. Padmanagara is polished stone, chalked ground designs, and saturated colour — a city where ornament is maintenance and maintenance is civic duty.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Lotus** — the national flower, in stone, in ponds, in floating lamps
- **Kolam/rangoli ground design** chalked at Mandala Chowk, asking feet to walk kindly
- Floating oil lamp on the river at dusk (never touch one — bow to it)
- Ash pinch on the wrist to seal an oath at Devalok Gate
- Brass cup shared before a verdict at the water courts
- Peacock feather and tiled peacock roof
- Sand tables and prayer wheels sharing space with abacuses at the River Colleges

## Pattern and ornament
- Mandala: concentric radial geometry — use it for UI panels, market floors, and item frames
- Kolam line-and-dot patterns, chalk-white on stone
- Paisley (buta) and floral block-print repeats on cloth
- Carved gateway registers: horizontal bands of small figures and foliage
- Mirror-work and sequins on festival textiles

## Materials and finish
Carved sandstone, polished brass, silk and cotton, marigold, powdered pigment, banana leaf, clay cups, sandalwood, mirror-glass. Finish: **kept and polished** — stone waxed by touch, brass gleaming, colour reapplied rather than allowed to fade.

## Architecture and silhouette
Terraced ghats stepping down to water like open palms, carved gateways, a colonnaded artisan arcade with tiled feather roofs, red-stone cloisters, temple shikhara towers. Silhouette: **stepped water edge below, clustered spires above**.

## Dress and accessories
Draped and wrapped cloth in saturated colour with block-printed borders, shoulder cloths, bangles, anklets with small bells, garlands worn and given. Scholars carry palm-leaf or bound texts; merchants carry cloth purses and quick fingers.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Banana-leaf thalis, clay chai cups, marigold garlands, brass lamps and vessels, pigment cones, veenas and miniature paintings from the Peacock Arcade, cinema posters brought back by the diaspora. Signage is **painted panel with hand-lettered script and a floral border**.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 6`): **Apsara, Gandharva, Klabuster, Naga, Palmyr, Peaflare, Temple Mantis**.

Celestial performers and temple guardians. Apsara and Gandharva are dancers and musicians — design them around motion and cloth, with anklet bells and layered silk. Naga gets carved-balustrade patterning; Peaflare is the peacock-fire creature carrying the identity palette; Temple Mantis is the small carved guardian brought to life, stone-grey with painted accents.

## Guardrails
- Colour should be **applied, not weathered** — this city repaints and re-chalks constantly.
- Public shade is a civic right; put trees, awnings, and colonnades in exteriors.
- The Dawnmarch card text is crude; the lore is warm and dignified. Follow the lore.
