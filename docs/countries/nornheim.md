## Design thesis
**Oaths keep the cold out.** Every visual element is either a fastening, a record, or a weave. Skeldgard is carved wood and blue ice, and the most distinctive design idea in the whole setting lives here: the **public loom**, where grievances are tied on as ribbons and woven into civic cloth.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Three loom-poles** and the woven cloth (the Nornweave) — the national emblem
- Grievance ribbon tied to a warp thread; red thread on a sleeve = an unkept promise
- Runes cut in slow, patient strokes; the **truth stave** worn at the throat
- Worldtree roots twisting from cliff and terrace like iron
- Breaching whale (Whale-Gate); the whale-rib harbour bell
- Shields hung as offerings on the Valkyrie Steps
- Hairline crack in vow-stone (shared grammar with Aegia Aeterna's vow-stones)

## Pattern and ornament
- Urnes-style gripping-beast interlace: long, thin, ribbon-like animals biting their own knots
- Warp-and-weft grids; herringbone and twill on textiles
- Runic bands along lintels, ship strakes, and item borders
- Knotwork that *ties* rather than merely repeats — knots should look functional
- Chip carving and kolrosing on small wooden goods

## Materials and finish
Split timber, tarred pine, felted wool, oiled leather, brass clasp-work, whale bone, amber, hammered iron, vow-stone (pale blue-veined). Surfaces are wind-scoured and salt-bleached; metals are dark and matte with bright wear on the edges.

## Architecture and silhouette
Longhalls with steep shake roofs and carved gable dragons; stone tiers ringing the Law Rock; ice-scored piers; cliff-side terraces braided with living roots. Silhouettes are **low, long, and braced** — everything leans into wind.

## Dress and accessories
Layered wool tunics and hooded cloaks, wolf-grey for the watch. Felted mittens, fur-trimmed hoods, cross-strapped boots. Brass and amber clasp-work at the shoulder, a small rune stave on a thong at the throat, braided hair with ribbon ties. Red sleeve-thread for anyone mid-promise.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Weregild ledgers, ribbon bundles, drinking horns, oil-and-amber lanterns, smoked fish on racks, ember-cheese stones, berry-cakes, carved charms. Signage is **runic-inflected**, carved and painted into wood, never printed.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 2`): **Fire Giant, Frost Giant, Kraken, Pineskrell, Ratatoskr, Skjoldram, Tarnhelm, Tin Squirrel**.

Two poles: elemental giants at huge scale, and small clever forest/hoard creatures. Give the small ones brass clasp-work, ribbon ties, and rune-etched accessories; give the giants geology (rime-crusted skin, glowing seams). Skjoldram is a shield-ram — lean into painted shield-plate.

## Landmarks to draw from
The Shieldring (thingstead) · Worldroot Terrace and the Public Loom · Whale-Gate Harbor · Hall of Runes · Valkyrie Steps · Oath-Ice Bridge · Hearthlane Market · the longhouse *Three Looms* · the Weeping Lanes (horror site)

## Guardrails
- No horned helmets. Ever.
- Keep interlace thin and continuous; thick rounded knotwork reads as Bretonreach.
- Cold palette everywhere except firelight — that warm/cold split is the whole mood.
