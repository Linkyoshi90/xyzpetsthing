## Design thesis
Sun-bleached civic marble that is *maintained*, not ruined. Everything is straight-edged, measured, and slightly too proud of itself — then softened by laundry lines, red sauce, and an argument about olive oil. Heliadora's design language is **order in stone, warmth in cloth and food**.

## Motifs & symbols
- Laurel wreath and bronze laurel branch (civic honour)
- The **vow-stone**: a palm-sized carved stone with a hairline crack — the empire's most specific icon, use it for oath/contract UI
- Sunbeam-turned-to-stone (a ray rendered as a fluted column)
- Speaker's Circle: concentric rings inlaid in a plaza floor
- Aegis shield with justice runes along the rim (Aegis Causeways ring like shields)

## Pattern & ornament
- Greek key / meander borders on hems, tile edges, and item frames
- Egg-and-dart and acanthus on capitals; keep carving crisp, never crumbled
- Black-figure vase silhouettes for decals and signage
- Mosaic tesserae in floors — small, irregular, warm-white with one accent colour
- Fluting: vertical grooves are the default surface texture for anything columnar

## Materials & finish
Polished white marble, bronze that has gone green only in the seams, terracotta, sun-faded linen awnings, olive wood, hammered brass. Finish is **clean and cared-for** — Heliadora fines you for a scuffed monument, so no moss, no vine-choked ruin except deliberately at the Abandoned Ruins site.

## Architecture & silhouette
Colonnades, pediments, wide shallow steps, roped-off stairs, open forums. Low horizontal masses punctuated by verticals. Domestic layer on top: shutters, balconies, drying laundry, trattoria awnings, string lights. Baths of Alba = white stone, steam, echo.

## Dress & accessories
Draped linen (chiton/toga read) in white, cream, and ochre with a single meander-banded hem. Laurel circlets for officials, bronze fibula clasps, leather sandals with wrapped straps. Modern-diaspora layer: aprons, rolled sleeves, flat caps, a tea towel over the shoulder.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Vow-stones, wax tablets, illuminated scrolls, bronze scales, amphorae, brick pizza ovens and peels, paper cones of fried anchovies, marble drinking fountains. Signage is **carved capitals**, all-caps, no lowercase, letters spaced wide.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 1`): **Aegis Boar, Centaur, Harpy, Lamia, Marmotaur, Pegasus, Strigowl, Talosling**.
Classical myth stock. Skin them with bronze, marble, and laurel: Talosling reads as animated bronze statuary with verdigris seams; Aegis Boar wears shield-plate; Pegasus and Strigowl get marble-white coats with gold leaf accents. Avoid dirt-and-rag fantasy — these creatures are civic property and look it.

## Landmarks to draw from
Forum Aeternum · Curia Solaris (Senate Hill) · Aegis Causeways · Baths of Alba · Sunmarket · the ruins of the old quarter · Pizzeria Sol Invicta · Library Annex (vow-stones)

## Guardrails
- Keep it **maintained antiquity**, not overgrown ruins — that contrast is the point.
- Don't blur into Kemet (no hieroglyph bands, no lotus) or Rheinland (no half-timber).
- The comedy is in the modern food layer sitting on perfect marble. Play it straight-faced.
