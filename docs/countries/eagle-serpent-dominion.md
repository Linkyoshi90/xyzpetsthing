## Design thesis
**Power is balance: sky and earth, blade and blossom.** A lake city of white stone islands and turquoise canals, where a plaza can hold a decree at noon and a flower offering at dusk. Every design decision should pair a hard thing with a soft thing — obsidian and petals, eagle and serpent, causeway and canoe.

## Motifs and symbols
- **Eagle and serpent sharing one standard** — the national device
- Painted serpent balustrades coiling down a stair or along a bridge rail
- Feather standard and feather-mosaic panel
- Copper bells (small, round, in clusters) on ankles and cargo
- Obsidian blade edge and obsidian mirror
- Red-painted couriers (do not block them) and the red thread tied to a wrist at market
- Chinampa: floating garden anchored by willow stakes

## Pattern and ornament
- Stepped-fret bands (greca) along cornices and hems — squarer and blockier than Itzam glyph work
- Feather mosaic: overlapping scale-like feather tiles in graded colour
- Family colour stripes painted on door lintels in the Canal Quarter
- Bark-paper (amatl) tally marks and glyph blocks
- Woven cloth "story" panels — each textile holds a whole narrative

## Materials and finish
Lime plaster over stone, painted wood rails, feathers, jade, copper, obsidian, reed, cotton cloth, cacao and maize as trade goods. Finish is **bright, clean, and freshly painted** — this is a working capital, not a ruin.

## Architecture and silhouette
Stepped twin temples framing a plaza, long causeways over water with lifting gatehouses, a sunken ballcourt, floating gardens at the edges. Silhouette: **flat-topped pyramids and long low horizontals cut by tall banner poles**.

## Dress and accessories
Cotton mantles knotted at the shoulder, feather headdresses by rank (not universal), jade ear spools, copper bell anklets, quilted armour for the guard, body paint for runners. Priests wear copal-stained cloth and carry censers.

## Objects, signage, item flavour
Copal censers, bark-paper codices, obsidian blades, copper bells, feather bundles, cacao gourds, maize cakes used as small change, flower garlands. Signage is **banner-and-colour**: follow the banners, not the words.

## Creature design cues
Native species (`region_id = 9`): **Ahuizotl, Cipactli, Jaglare, Macawtl, Obsidian Macuahuitl Hound, Ocelot, Quetzalcoatl**.

Martial and ceremonial. Give predators feather-mosaic markings and jade or copper hardware; Obsidian Macuahuitl Hound is the design keystone — a beast whose body edges are set with obsidian blades like the weapon it is named for. Quetzalcoatl should be a *ceremony*, not a monster: feather gradients, turquoise, and slow coils.

## Guardrails
- Keep it **martial and civic**, not sacrificial-horror. The lore's tone is proud, organised, and slightly bureaucratic.
- Separate from Itzam Empire: ESD is lake-and-causeway with feather standards; Itzam is limestone-ridge with glyph stelae.
- Bright plaster is the base note — avoid the desaturated "lost civilisation" look entirely.
